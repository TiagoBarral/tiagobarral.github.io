Hi—I'm Tiago.

I'm a developer in Lisbon, learning out loud. I build production-minded side projects, I document the messy middle, and I write the notes I wish I had earlier.

This site is part portfolio, part working journal. I use it to think — to put scrappy systems in front of real constraints and figure out what they want to become.

What I'm interested in.

  • Agentic AI in real, narrow workflows — not chat for the sake of chat
  • Backend systems that admit they might fail (queues, retries, idempotency)
  • The boring decisions that decide whether software actually ships
  • Writing as a tool for thinking, not for the timeline

How I work.

Slowly. Honestly. With a strong preference for boring tools and small, observable steps. I'd rather ship a rough thing twice than a polished thing once.

— Tiago Barral
